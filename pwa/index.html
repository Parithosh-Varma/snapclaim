  • Coins seen at an angle correct for tilt, up to 60°
  • No coin means percentages only, never made-up millimetres

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Open an issue on our GitHub and we'll answer there.

Ask on GitHub
Do I need an account?

No. Open the site on your phone, tap Start a claim, and allow the camera. You can install it to your home screen if you'll use it again.

Why a coin?

A photo has no sense of size on its own. A coin has a known diameter, so once the app finds it, it can turn pixels into millimetres. If the coin is seen at an angle, its oval shape tells the app how much to correct.

What if I don't have a coin?

Switch to No coin before you take the photo. The report then gives the damage as a share of the wall instead of millimetres. It will never invent a size it can't measure.

Where does my photo go?

The photo is sent to the SnapClaim server so the damage can be outlined and measured, and the report written. A second outline is drawn on your own device to cross-check it. The app never keeps photos in its offline cache. Read the privacy notice for the details.

What does "Disputed" mean?

A second model tried to reproduce that number from the receipts and couldn't. The number stays in the report, marked in red, so whoever reads it knows to look closer.

Is this an official inspection?

No. SnapClaim gives you measured evidence to support a claim or a conversation. For structural concerns, talk to a qualified engineer.

What kinds of damage can it measure?

Cracks, stains, peeling and dents on walls and other flat surfaces. Cracks get a length and width; other damage gets an area and a share of the surface.
