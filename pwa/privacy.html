Privacy Policy — v2026-10-29-v3

Effective 29 Oct 2026. Photos stay on your phone unless you explicitly share, export, cross-check, or separately opt in to donate.

Default: nothing on servers

Cases live in IndexedDB on your device. No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no background uploads, no push.

What leaves the device (your tap only)

Measure: mask outlines (RLE), not photos. Cross-check: photo once to SAM 3.1. Render/share: report you asked for. Donate (separate opt-in, off by default): 768px JPEG, EXIF stripped, label, coin size, transcript, masks/receipts JSON, truncated user-agent → D1/R2 dataset store. Voice (live): ≤10s mono WAV for transcription only.

Consent and control

Donation is unticked by default and separate from Terms acceptance. Untick in Settings stops future uploads. Settings → Delete all cases wipes the device; contact support in Settings → Legal for server donation deletion by donation id. Retention: device until you delete; donations 24 months.
