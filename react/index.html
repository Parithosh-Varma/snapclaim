Early preview, in development

Damage you can prove.

Take one photo of a crack, dent or stain with a coin beside it. SnapClaim measures it to the millimeter and builds a report your landlord, insurer or repair shop can check for themselves.

See a real measurement Try the live app How it works

One real terrace crack, measured three ways: 227 mm tape span, 274 mm pin-to-pin path, Snapclaim 232 mm. Full 5-page report below.

  • Just a phone and a coin
  • Describe it out loud, no typing
  • Location data removed from every photo
  • Free to try — no account, no install

When something gets damaged, it's usually your word against theirs. A photo shows that it happened. SnapClaim shows how much, in numbers anyone can recheck.

For the moments it matters

Renters

Record the crack on move-in day, with its size. At move-out, there's no argument about whether it grew or who caused it.

Bike and car owners

A scrape in a parking lot or a dent after a service. Measure it before you hand over the keys or file with insurance.

Small shops

Water stains from a leak upstairs, or damage from a delivery. Show the owner or the insurer exactly how much was affected.
