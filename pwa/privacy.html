Privacy

Privacy notice

SnapClaim is a beta. This page says plainly what happens to your photo, your voice and your words when you make a report.

Last updated 29 October 2026

What we handle

  • The photo you take, and the points you tap on it.
  • The one-line description you type or say.
  • The measurements and the written report made from them.

There are no accounts, so we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

What stays on your device

  • The camera preview and the coin finder run in your browser.
  • A second outline of the damage is drawn on your device, to cross-check the server's outline.
  • Speech is turned into text on your device first. Only if that fails is the audio clip sent to our server to be transcribed.
  • The app's offline cache holds the app itself, never your photos.

What is sent to our server

To outline, measure and describe the damage, the photo, your taps and your description are sent to the SnapClaim server. The server passes them to the AI models that outline the damage, write the summary and check the numbers. When you create a report file, the photo is included in that request so it can be drawn into the file.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't add trackers or analytics to the app.
  • We don't send your report to anyone. You choose who gets it.

Your choices

You can use the No coin option, skip the microphone and type instead, and close the app at any point before a report is made. If you have a question about your data, open an issue on our GitHub.

This notice describes the beta as it runs today. If what we do changes, we'll update this page and its date first.
