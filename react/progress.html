Milestones

  1. Oct 31, 2026

    Known-truth bench

    Calibrated crack measurements checked against ground truth; width and area fixed on tilted walls.

  2. Nov 1, 2026

    Hardening sprint

    Tiered rate limits, four vulnerability-fix rounds and a top-20 bug sweep: receipts, spend caps and evidence gates throughout.

  3. Nov 1, 2026

    App ships as an installable PWA

    Capture flow, photo upload and voice input; admin panel behind Cloudflare Access; production at snapclaim.pages.dev.

  4. Nov 1, 2026

    Marketing site goes live

    This site launches on its own branch, with its own photos and its own privacy-respecting analytics: none.

  5. Nov 2, 2026

    First real case study published

    A terrace-slab crack measured at 218.5 mm against a 227 mm tape — inside ±10%, where a photo-alone guess was 39% off.

  6. Nov 2, 2026

    One page becomes five

    Home, How it works, Case study, FAQ — and this page, which reads the repo live.
