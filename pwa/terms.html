Terms

Terms of use

The short version: SnapClaim measures what it can see, shows its working, and is not a substitute for a professional inspection.

Last updated 29 October 2026

A beta product

SnapClaim is free to use while it's in beta. Features may change or stop working, and the service may be unavailable at times.

Not an inspection

A SnapClaim report is evidence to support a claim or a conversation. It is not a structural, engineering, legal or insurance assessment. If you're worried about safety, contact a qualified professional.

About the numbers

  • Measurements are computed from your photo and depend on its quality, the coin you used and where you tapped.
  • A second model checks each number. Values it can't reproduce are marked Disputed, and stay in the report so readers can judge them.
  • Without a coin, sizes are given as a share of the surface, not in millimetres.

Using it fairly

Only photograph property you're allowed to, and don't use SnapClaim to mislead anyone about damage. Don't try to break, overload or misuse the service.

Your reports

Reports you make are yours to share as you choose. The sample photo on our site is by Brina Blum on Unsplash.
